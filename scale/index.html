FAQ

When do credits expire?

Six months from purchase. Unused credits are non-refundable, non-transferable and have no cash value.

Is it a monthly payment?

No. It's a single upfront payment.

Are these the same as Higgsfield credits?

No. Our credits cover production: creative adaptation, asset prep, editing, QC and delivery. Higgsfield and other generation usage is funded separately by you.

How are revisions handled?

Each variation includes one revision round. A new hook, offer, language or direction may use another credit.

Does it guarantee another winner?

No ad result can be guaranteed. The pack helps you extend and test a pattern that's already working while it still performs.
